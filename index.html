About This Map

Physical attacks against crypto holders have increased significantly. This map aggregates all publicly known incidents to help you understand geographic risk.

This is not financial advice. It is a personal safety resource.

Data Source

Incident data from Jameson Lopp's Physical Bitcoin Attacks database (Unlicense). Severity classification inspired by Haseeb Qureshi's analysis methodology.

Limitations

  • Reporting bias: Attacks in countries with active English-language crypto media are more likely documented.
  • Zero incidents does not equal safe: Gray countries may simply lack reporting.
  • Conflict zones are flagged based on US State Dept Level 4 advisories regardless of crypto-specific data.

This map is for informational purposes only and does not constitute personal safety, legal, or financial advice.
